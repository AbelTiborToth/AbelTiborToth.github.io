Pricing

Every game is priced individually.

Send me the rulebook and I'll prepare a quote tailored to your game.

The price is based on

  • Game complexity
  • Project timeline
  • Additional feature requests

Payment options

Games on BGA's premium model generate a monthly revenue share for their publisher. Instead of paying the full fee, you can pay a reduced fee and share part of that monthly revenue with me.

Fixed fee

  • One-time payment, due after release.
  • You decide whether the game is free-to-play or premium on BGA.
  • On the premium model, you keep your full publisher share.

Lower fee + revenue share

  • Reduced one-time payment, due after release, based on the game's expected success on BGA.
  • An agreed percentage of your monthly premium revenue share.
  • The game stays on the premium model for an agreed period.
  • Only BGA premium revenue is shared. Sales of physical copies are not affected.

BGA keeps half of the premium revenue a game earns. The other half is the publisher share.

More information about BGA for publishers

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to contact BGA before requesting a quote?

No. You can request a quote without contacting BGA. Later in the project, you'll need to be in touch with BGA to test the adaptation and to give the necessary licence permissions. More information about BGA for publishers

How long does an adaptation take?

It depends on the scope of the game. Lighter games take less time than complex ones, and alpha and beta testing also need to be planned in. You'll receive an estimated timeline together with the quote.

What do you need from me?

For a quote, only the rulebook. Once we've reached an agreement, I'll need the game's graphic files and a contact person on your side who can test and review the adaptation.

Can I follow the progress?

Yes. Until the adaptation is playable, I usually share screenshots or videos of my progress. Once it's playable, you can test it on BGA and send me feedback. Nothing is released without your approval.

What's the difference between free-to-play and premium?

Anyone on BGA can start a free-to-play game. A premium game can only be started by a BGA Premium member, but anyone can join it. Premium games earn the publisher a share of BGA's premium revenue. Publishers can switch between the two models at any time. More information about BGA for publishers

Do BGA or you take ownership of the game?

No. We only use a licence, the game stays yours. More information about BGA for publishers

Can the one-time fee be zero euro with the lower fee + revenue share option?

Yes, but only if we can say with enough confidence that the game will be a huge success on BGA. In that case, it's possible to pay through the revenue share only.

Do you handle marketing as well?

With the lower fee + revenue share option, we can also agree on limited marketing support.

Can you add expansions later?

Yes. Expansions are quoted separately, like a new game, and usually cost less because the base game is already implemented.

How does the discount for multiple games work?

When several games or expansions are commissioned together, I quote them as a single combined agreement, at a lower total than quoting each one separately. The exact discount depends on the games and is included in the quote.

What happens after the 3 months of maintenance?

While we're working together on another game, maintenance continues for as long as that project is active. After that, we can agree on ongoing maintenance, or on fixes and updates as needed.
